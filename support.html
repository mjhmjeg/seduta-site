Getting started

The first transcription needs the speech model. Seduta downloads it once (about 470 MB). On the iPhone it waits for Wi-Fi unless you allow cellular downloads in Settings. Until then the home screen shows a Download button.

Recording a call on the Mac. Leave “Ask to record calls” on. When Zoom, Teams, Meet or another app opens the microphone, a notification offers to record. Or press Start Recording yourself. Keep Seduta open at login (Settings › General) so it is there when the call starts.

Recording a call on the iPhone. iOS gives phone calls exclusive use of the microphone, so Seduta records in-person meetings. Record a call in the Phone app; when it ends, share the recording from Notes to Seduta and it becomes a meeting.

Summaries

  • “Turn on Apple Intelligence”: on-device summaries need Apple Intelligence on, in System Settings on a Mac with Apple silicon or an iPhone 15 Pro or later with iOS 26. Otherwise use an endpoint.
  • “The endpoint refused the API key”: check the key in Settings › Summaries. EUrouter keys start with a dashboard at eurouter.ai; the Settings screen shows what a key has spent.
  • Your own server: any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works, including Ollama on your Mac at http://localhost:11434/v1. Lower “Words per request” for small models.

Speakers and names

  • Your own voice is “Me”. Other people start as “Speaker 1, 2, …”. Tap a name in the transcript to rename it; Seduta remembers the voice for next time.
  • Names and terms the transcriber gets wrong go in the names-and-terms file (Settings › Storage). Names from calendar invites are added by themselves.

Files

Every meeting is a folder in iCloud Drive › Seduta: meta.json, transcript.md, summary.md, and the recording until it is removed. Deleted recordings sit in “Recently Deleted” for a few days. You can open, copy or delete anything there; Seduta only reads what it finds.
