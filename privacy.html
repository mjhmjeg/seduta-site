Privacy

Seduta has no account and no server that sees your meetings. This page lists every place data goes, so you can check the claim rather than take it.

SedutaPrivacySupport

On your devices

Recordings, transcripts, summaries, meeting details from your calendar (title, participants, notes), the voice prints Seduta learns to recognise people, and your names-and-terms list are files in iCloud Drive › Seduta. iCloud Drive is Apple's service under your Apple Account; it is encrypted by Apple, and end-to-end encrypted when you turn on Advanced Data Protection. Settings sync through iCloud, and an API key you enter is kept in the Keychain and travels with iCloud Keychain.

Transcription and speaker separation run entirely on the device. Audio never leaves it for that.

This website

seduta.spert.ai counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no tracking across sites, no personal profile. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, sees each request (IP address, browser) as the host anyway. The waitlist is the only form; it is in the table above.

Recordings and consent

Recording is your call. Seduta reminds you next to every record control to let everyone know they are being recorded, and posts a notification on your Mac when a recording starts. Whether recording a conversation is lawful where you are is your responsibility.

Retention

By default the recording moves to “Recently Deleted” inside the Seduta folder 48 hours after it is transcribed, and is erased 72 hours after that. Both windows are settings; 0 keeps recordings. Transcripts and summaries stay until you delete the meeting.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record. Asked the first time you start a recording.
  • System audio (Mac): to record the other side of a call.
  • Calendar: to name meetings, list participants and remind you before a call. Asked from the Upcoming section, and optional.
  • Notifications (Mac): to offer to record a call and to tell you when a summary is ready.
