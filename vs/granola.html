Where Seduta is different

  • Transcribed on the device
  • No account needed
  • One-off purchase or your own key
  • AI agents read your notes on your Mac, not through a cloud server

Where Granola is stronger

  • Runs on Windows as well as Mac
  • Integrations such as Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Zapier and an MCP connector
  • Calendar-driven notifications and team sharing with workspaces
  • Notes on outgoing phone calls placed from the Granola iPhone app (not incoming calls)

Record on your Mac, iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. No bot, no account.
